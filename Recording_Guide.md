## Step 1: Record the real game on your laptop (no headset needed)

**Option A: easiest (mouse)**
1. Open your GitHub Pages link in Chrome on the laptop. Make it full screen (F11).
2. Start the screen recorder:
   - Windows: press **Win + Alt + R** (Xbox Game Bar), or use OBS Studio (free).
   - Mac: press **Cmd + Shift + 5** and choose "Record entire screen".
3. Click the orbs with the mouse. In Look & Pinch mode, aim with the middle of the screen and press **Space** to pinch.

**Option B: looks more like VR (headset emulator)**
1. In Chrome, install **Immersive Web Emulator** from the Chrome Web Store. It's Meta's WebXR emulator for Quest.
2. Open your game link, press F12 (DevTools), and open the **WebXR** tab.
3. Click **Enter VR** in the game. Switch the emulator to hand mode, then move the hands and use poke or pinch on the orbs.
4. Record the screen as in Option A.

## Step 2: What to record (follow this order and match the voiceover)

The voiceover has 5 parts with a 2.5-second pause between each part.

1. **Menu (8 s):** "Here is the game running in the browser. This is the menu. Every option is an orb you touch with your fingertip." → Show the menu and hover near the orbs.
2. **Classic mode (12 s):** "I start Classic mode. Every orb plays a note, and my combo builds the music. At combo ten, the bass kicks in and the sky lights up." → Click Classic 60s and pop cyan orbs quickly to reach combo 10.
3. **Gold and red (7 s):** "Gold orbs play a chord. Dark red orbs break the music, so I avoid them." → Pop a gold orb and avoid a dark red one.
4. **Daily Challenge (8 s):** "Now the Daily Challenge. My best run of the day comes back as a ghost hand, and I race it." → **Before recording**, play one Daily Challenge round so a ghost gets saved. Then record a second round, where the violet ghost hand appears.
5. **Look & Pinch (9 s):** "Finally, Look and Pinch. I aim by looking at an orb, and pinch to pop it." → In the menu, turn on Look & Pinch, start a round, aim the centre of the screen at orbs and press Space.

Turn the game sound **on** while recording. The real music under the voiceover sounds great.

## Step 3: Join in CapCut (phone or PC)
1. New project → add `Part1_Intro.mp4`, then your recording, then `Part3_Benefits_Limits_Future_Ending.mp4`.
2. Add `Part2_Demo_Voiceover.mp3` as audio under your recording. Cut or move each part so it matches what's on screen.
3. Lower your recording's own volume to about 30% so the voice is clear.
4. Export at 1080p, then upload to YouTube as Public.

Tip: For a more natural voice, paste these lines into CapCut's text-to-speech and use that instead of the MP3.
