Lumen Pop: Neon Symphony: open in the Meta Quest Browser and tap “Enter VR”. No controllers needed. On desktop, click the orbs (Space = pinch in Look & Pinch mode).
How to play and what is inside Poke glowing cyan orbs with your index finger: every pop plays the next note of a melody, and your combo adds layers to the music (hi-hats at x5, bass and a light show at x10, an arpeggio at x20). Gold orbs play a chord and are worth more. Dark red orbs break the music and cost points.

Modes: Classic (60 s), Zen (no timer, no hazards), Daily Challenge (same orb sequence for everyone today, and a ghost hand replays your best run of the day so you can race it).

Options: “Large targets” makes orbs bigger and slower. “Look & Pinch” lets you aim with where you look (head gaze) and pinch thumb and index finger to pop, so you can play without reaching. Everything is playable with hands only, seated, within a two-foot radius.
